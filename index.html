Data methodology & limitations

  • Prices: Yahoo Finance/yfinance completed US sessions; no intraday close presented as final.
  • ETF flows: Curated ETF.com daily observations. Not automatically harvested, not a full-week flow series. They do not satisfy the green gate.
  • Dated trades: Curated ARK tracker observations; cannot generalize to the market. ARK publishes its own notices at ARK Funds.
  • SEC: Automated EDGAR 13F information table comparison for 15 managers. Failing or missing manager filings are excluded, not estimated. Snapshot dates Q2 30 June and Q3 30 September 2026.
  • Freshness: A successful GitHub Action updates prices and SEC when accessible. ETF flows/trades need source-linked curation.
