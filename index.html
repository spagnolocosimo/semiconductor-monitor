How the traffic light works

RED: Both market benchmarks are deteriorating or the three-week recovery gate is not met with clear bearish price evidence.

YELLOW: Stabilization is developing, but flow/trade confirmation is missing, mixed or insufficient.

GREEN: Three consecutive completed weeks without a new 13-week low in BOTH ETFs and above their respective 4-week average, positive verified flows for at least two of those weeks, and two independently sourced dated net-buying events by major investors in the watchlist.

UNKNOWN: Data unavailable or stale. A missing flow is not zero. An institutional holding increase is not a dated transaction.

Scheduled update: weekdays at 22:00 Europe/Amsterdam. US markets may still be open at that time; the site uses the most recent completed US trading session, never an unfinished session.

Public price feed: Yahoo Finance via yfinance, not an exchange-certified feed. SEC rows are verified snapshots. ETF flows and fund trades require sourced input; the system never invents them.

Research tool, not investment advice. Data can be delayed or incomplete. Always check source links and update timestamp before acting.
